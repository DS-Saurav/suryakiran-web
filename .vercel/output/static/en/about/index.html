About Us

Architects of Infrastructure

Surya Kiran Renewable Energy Pvt. Ltd. stands as a trusted, domestic partner for planning, designing, building, and maintaining the infrastructure and energy systems that underpin Nepal's growth. Based in Birendranagar, Surkhet, in Karnali Province, we deliver civil engineering, renewable energy, strategic consultancy, and construction supervision — all integrated, all accountable.

Our Mission

To plan, design, build, and maintain the infrastructure and energy systems that underpin Nepal's growth — from first survey to final commissioning and beyond.

Our Vision

To become Nepal's most trusted name in infrastructure development — where quality, transparency, and local expertise are the foundation of every project.

Our Values

Accountability: We stand behind every deliverable. Integrity: Compliance with government standards and environmental commitments is our baseline. Local: Nepali-built, Nepali-operated, engineered for Nepali conditions.

Our Journey

Milestones

2008

Surya Kiran founded, focusing on civil engineering consultancy in Karnali Province.

2012

First major government infrastructure project delivered — roads and drainage in Birendranagar.

2016

Renewable energy division expanded: solar rooftop, water pumping, and system fabrication.

2020

Expanded services to all seven provinces; over 200 projects delivered.

2024

Established as a full-service infrastructure and energy firm in Karnali Province.
